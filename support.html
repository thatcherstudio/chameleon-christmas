What the game is

Chameleon Christmas is a gentle hidden-object game. The tree is bigger than the screen, so you look around it to find 20 ornaments tucked into the branches, along with the chameleon from Chameleon Jump. Every time you spot him, he hides again a little better: first in a loud color out in the open, later in just the right green behind the needles. There is no timer and no way to lose.

How to play

  1. Start. Tap the title screen.
  2. Look around. Drag to move around the tree, and pinch to zoom in and out. The magnifier button at the top right switches between the whole tree and a closer look.
  3. Hang the ornaments. Tap an ornament in the branches to hang it. The tray along the bottom fills in as you go.
  4. Find the chameleon. Tap him when you see him. He pops out, then slips away to a new hiding spot. Each time you find him, another string of lights comes on.
  5. The star. When the last ornament is up, he grabs the star. Find him one more time to put it on top and light the whole tree.
  6. Play again. Tap the end screen to decorate a fresh tree. He chooses new hiding spots every round.

Questions

I can't find the chameleon. I'm stuck!

Wait a moment and the game will help you:

  • After about 8 seconds he starts to blink.
  • After 20 seconds the branches over him rustle.
  • After 40 seconds an arrow at the edge of the screen points his way. If he is already on screen, a ring pulses around him instead.

It also helps to tap the magnifier button at the top right to see the whole tree at once.

I can't find the last ornament.

Every few seconds an ornament that is still hidden gives a little glint. Watch for the sparkle. The tray along the bottom shows a faint silhouette of each ornament you haven't found yet, so you know what shape to look for.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button at the top right of the screen. The game remembers your choice next time. The game is also silent whenever your iPhone or iPad is in silent mode.

Is there anything to buy?

No. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads. The whole game is there from the start.

Does it need the internet or an account?

No. The game works fully offline, there is nothing to sign in to, and it collects no data. See the privacy policy.

What does the number at the top mean?

It counts how many times you have spotted the chameleon this round. When the tree is lit you'll see your total and your best round so far. Only the best score is saved, and it stays on your device.

Why isn't the snow falling?

If Reduce Motion is turned on in your device's Accessibility settings, the game turns off the falling snow, the sparkle bursts and the swinging ornaments. Everything else works the same.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 16 or later. The game plays in portrait (upright).
