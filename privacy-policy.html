Chameleon Christmas

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

The short version: Chameleon Christmas collects no information about you or anyone who plays it. It never connects to the internet, and it has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and nothing to buy.

Who we are

Chameleon Christmas is a hidden-object game for iPhone and iPad made by Andrew Watkins ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information or usage data. The app contains no code that connects to the internet, so it cannot send anything from your device to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The app saves two small settings in your device's standard storage for apps:

  • your best score (the most times you have spotted the chameleon in one round), and
  • whether the sound is on or off.

These stay on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your device, they may be included in your own backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code of any kind. It does not use advertising identifiers or track you across other apps or websites. Because we share no information with anyone, there are no third parties to hold to this policy.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or anything else on your device. It plays sounds and gentle vibrations, and that's all.

Children's privacy

Chameleon Christmas is made to be enjoyed by the whole family, including young children. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. A child cannot type in a name, create an account, chat, share anything or be shown ads, because none of those features exist in the app.

Because nothing is collected, there is no personal information for a parent or guardian to review, correct or delete. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, please write to us at the address below.

Apple and the App Store

You get the app through Apple's App Store, which handles your download under Apple's privacy policy. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous, combined crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems in the game.

Keeping and deleting information

We hold no information about you, so there is nothing for us to keep or for you to ask us to delete. To remove the best score and sound setting, delete the app from your device.

If you email us, we will see your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we never add you to a mailing list or share it. You can ask us to delete our conversation at any time.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever handles information differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email andy@awatkins.io.
